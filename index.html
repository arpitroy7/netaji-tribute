🇮🇳 Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose

Introduction

Subhas Chandra Bose, popularly known as Netaji, was one of the most influential leaders of India’s independence movement. His courage, determination, and strong leadership inspired millions of Indians to fight against British rule.

Early Life & Education

Netaji was born on 23 January 1897 in Cuttack, Odisha. He was a brilliant student and completed his education in India and England. He passed the Indian Civil Services (ICS) examination but resigned to serve the nation instead of working under British rule.

Role in the Freedom Struggle

Subhas Chandra Bose strongly believed that India needed complete independence. He became president of the Indian National Congress but later formed the Forward Bloc due to ideological differences.

During World War II, he formed the Indian National Army (INA) to fight against British forces. His famous slogan was:

"Give me blood, and I will give you freedom!"

Major Contributions

  • Leader of Indian National Army (INA)
  • Promoted armed struggle for independence
  • Inspired youth with patriotic speeches
  • Established Azad Hind Government

Legacy

Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose remains a symbol of bravery and patriotism. His vision of a free and strong India continues to inspire generations. Every year, 23 January is celebrated as Parakram Diwas in his honor.
